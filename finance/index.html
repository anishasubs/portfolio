AI advisor

    
Ask an AI where to put your money, using the balances already in this app.


    

      This one feature sends your financial summary off this device.
      Everything else in Ledger stays local. Turning this on means that when you press Ask —
      and only then — a summary of your finances is sent to your own proxy at
      , which forwards it to OpenAI.
    


    
What gets sent

    
    
      
  • Account names as you typed them, their types, balances and APYs
    • 
      
  • Monthly spending totals by category, income, and your savings rate
    • 
      
  • Budgets, goals, and your 401(k) settings
    • 
      
  • Anything you type into the profile fields below
    • 
    


    
What never gets sent

    
    
      
  • Individual transactions or merchant names — only category totals
    • 
      
  • Account numbers — Ledger never stores them in the first place
    • 
      
  • Anything at all until you press Ask. Enabling this sends nothing by itself.
    • 
    


    
You can see the exact payload before every send, and turn this back off at any time.

How your data is handled

    
Worth knowing, since this is real financial data.

    
    
      
  • This page loads nothing from the internet — no external scripts, fonts, or stylesheets, and no network requests of any kind. You can verify that in your browser's Network tab, or run it fully offline.
    • 
      
  • PDF reading and screenshot OCR use libraries stored in finance/vendor/ on your own disk. They are loaded only when you actually import a file, and they run entirely inside this page.
    • 
      
  • Files you drop — CSV, PDF, or image — are read in-page with the browser's FileReader. Nothing is ever uploaded, and no statement is sent to any service for processing.
    • 
      
  • Every insight on the Overview tab is computed locally by rules in this file, and OCR runs on your machine rather than through a vision API.
    • 
      
  • The one exception is the AI advisor, which is off unless you turn it on. When enabled and you press Ask, a summary — balances, category totals, budgets, goals, 401(k) settings — is sent to the endpoint you configure. Individual transactions and merchant names are never included, and you can inspect the exact payload before every send. Turn it off and the page goes back to making no network requests at all.
    • 
      
  • Data is stored per-origin. Opening this file from disk and from a web address gives you two separate stores — use the JSON backup to move between them.
    • 
      
  • Anyone with access to this browser profile can read the data. Don't use a shared machine.
