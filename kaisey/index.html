The Problem

The Triple Bind

MBA students juggle 60+ weekly commitments across three competing priorities — and the tools they rely on don't talk to each other. Google Calendar handles recruiting. Canvas tracks academics. Club events live in GroupMe. The result: constant context-switching, missed deadlines, and a creeping sense that something important is always falling through the cracks.

When priorities shift — grades slipping right as recruiting peaks — realigning everything manually is a recipe for burnout.

📚

Academics

Classes, team projects, cold calls, exams — tracked in Canvas but disconnected from everything else.

💼

Recruiting

Networking coffees, applications, firm events, interview prep — scattered across Google Calendar and spreadsheets.

🎉

Social

Club leadership, events, relationships — the stuff that makes MBA life worth it, always the first to get cut.

Product Thinking

Key Decisions

Why OAuth over email/password?

Reduces friction — MBA students already live in Google. Enables instant calendar integration on first login. No new password to remember, no verification email to wait for.

Why a toggle, not manual task ranking?

MBA priorities shift weekly. A toggle maps to how students actually think: "This week is about recruiting" vs. ranking 47 individual tasks. One interaction, total rewrite of recommendations.

Privacy-first architecture

Zero permanent storage of calendar data. All processing happens in real-time. Users can revoke access anytime via Google. Trust is the product — especially with personal schedule data.
