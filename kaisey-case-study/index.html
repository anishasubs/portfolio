The Problem

The Quadruple Bind

Students juggle four competing priorities every single day — academics, recruiting, social life, and wellness. The tools they use are just as fragmented as the demands on their time.

📚

Academics

Canvas deadlines, group projects, reading lists — all in one silo with no connection to the rest of your schedule.

💼

Recruiting

Coffee chats, application deadlines, interview prep — scattered across email, spreadsheets, and calendar invites.

🎉

Social Life

Club events, networking dinners, weekend trips — the first thing to go when the workload spikes, but the hardest to get back.

💚

Wellness

Gym sessions, sleep, mental health breaks — always deprioritized until burnout hits, then suddenly the most important thing.

The result? Disconnected tools, context-switching burnout, and a constant feeling that something important is slipping through the cracks.

The Solution

What I Built

Kaisey is an AI chief of staff that connects your calendar, coursework, and career goals into a single intelligent layer — then helps you decide what matters most, right now.

⚡

Dynamic Priority Toggle

Slide between Academics, Recruiting, Social, and Wellness mode. Kaisey instantly re-ranks your day based on what matters most this week.

🔗

Connected Stack (MCP)

Google Calendar, Canvas, and email wired together through a Model Context Protocol — so Kaisey sees your full picture, not just fragments.

📖

Student Playbook (RAG)

Retrieval-augmented generation trained on academic scheduling heuristics — Kaisey knows that finals week and recruiting week are different beasts.

Product Decisions

Key Product Decisions

Authentication

OAuth over Email/Password

Google OAuth gives instant calendar access with zero onboarding friction. Students sign in once and Kaisey is already reading their schedule. No extra setup, no permissions pop-ups later.

Prioritization

Toggle over Manual Ranking

A simple four-mode selector (Academics / Recruiting / Social / Wellness) beats a complex manual ranking system. One tap shifts the entire priority stack — fast enough to switch mid-day.

Architecture

Privacy-First Design

Calendar data is processed in-session and never stored on our servers. The AI sees what it needs to prioritize, then forgets. Students control their data completely.
